# DuckLabs (company behind DuckDB) joins AWS

## Summary

- DuckLabs, the company behind DuckDB, joins Amazon Web Services. It was announced 2026-08-26 and completed 2026-08-31, effective 2026-09-01.
- DuckDB, DuckLake and Quack remain MIT-licensed and stewarded by the nonprofit DuckDB Foundation. The Foundation adds a technical advisory board.
- Financial terms were not disclosed.

## What Happened

- The announcement was authored by Mark Raasveldt and Hannes Mühleisen. The team stays together in Amsterdam.
- They stated a plan to open the extension stack so extensions signed by third parties can run in DuckDB.

## What Changed

- The corporate owner of DuckDB's core team is now AWS. The open-source license and Foundation governance stay as stated.

## Why It Matters

- DuckDB is a core local-analytics engine. Ownership by a hyperscaler may shift roadmap priorities; this is interpretation, not stated.
- DuckDB 2.0 ("Cyanoptera") is in alpha with GA planned for October 2026, per the MotherDuck newsletter.

## Practical Impact

- No immediate change for users; the license is unchanged.

## Watch Next

- DuckDB 2.0 GA (October 2026).
- Composition of the Foundation's technical advisory board.
- Extension signing changes.
