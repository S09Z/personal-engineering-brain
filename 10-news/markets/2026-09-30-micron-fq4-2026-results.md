# Micron fiscal Q4 2026: record $54.23B revenue, guides $61.5B for next quarter

## Summary

- Micron reported fiscal Q4 2026 revenue of $54.23B (quarter ended 2026-09-03), up from $41.46B in the prior quarter and $11.32B a year earlier.
- GAAP gross margin was 86.8%; GAAP net income $37.70B; GAAP diluted EPS $32.87.
- Company guidance for fiscal Q1 2027: revenue $61.5B ± $1.5B.

## What Happened

All figures below are from Micron's press release filed with the SEC on 2026-09-30.

Fiscal Q4 2026:

| | GAAP | Non-GAAP |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin | 86.8% | 87.0% |
| Net income | $37.70B | $38.40B |
| Diluted EPS | $32.87 | $33.42 |

- Operating cash flow: $43.97B (prior quarter $25.39B; year ago $5.73B).
- Revenue by business unit:
  - Core Data Center: $18.00B
  - Cloud Memory: $16.28B
  - Mobile & Client: $13.11B
  - Automotive & Embedded: $6.82B

Full fiscal year 2026:

- Revenue $133.19B (fiscal 2025: $37.38B).
- GAAP net income $84.97B; GAAP diluted EPS $74.33.
- Net capital expenditures $27.37B, of which $10.77B in Q4.

## What Changed

Company guidance for fiscal Q1 2027 (Micron's own outlook, not a forecast by this note):

- Revenue: $61.5B ± $1.5B
- Gross margin: about 85.95% GAAP, 86.25% non-GAAP
- Diluted EPS: $37.84 ± $1.00 GAAP, $38.15 ± $1.00 non-GAAP

## Why It Matters

- Memory is a direct read on AI data-center demand: the two data-center units are $34.28B of the $54.23B quarter.
- Interpretation, not stated in the filing: revenue nearly five times the year-ago quarter at an ~87% gross margin points to very tight memory supply and pricing power.
